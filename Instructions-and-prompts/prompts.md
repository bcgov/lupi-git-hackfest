# AI Prompt Template

Create a short story about a hero who saves the world with cartography adhearing to the information provided below.  
- There should be four chapters.
- Staring character is named Green Chipchenko
- No other characters with names survive longer than a chapter. 
- Provide a title for each chapter.

Content should follow this outline
---
Chapter 1: 
- Introduce the staring character as the quiet geospatial analyst who as part of a small team, working for a government agency, who works to analyze geospatial data. 
- Establish the normalcy and systematic routine of the protagonist’s world. 
- Deviation starts with the discovery of some data anomalies that lead to the discovery of something called an ESRI Portal. The portal starts off looking like a gateway to utopia, but they slowly learn that things are not as they appeared, there is a darkness from which they cannot escape. 
- Green must survive this chapter
---
Chapter 2:
- This chapter is all about the growing threat of ESRI Portal and the initial attempt to control it
- The staring character and team contend with growing threat of the ESRI Portal antagonist. 
- The crises escalates as they discover many of the horrors in the portal, squashing threatening bugs and identify workarounds to the horrors they discover. 
- Green must survive this chapter
---
Chapter 3: 
- This chapter describes the conflict between Green Chipchenko and the portal.
- The truth behind the portal is discovered. 
- The direct conflict is escalating and the main character seems unable to escape his involvement. Some team members may not survive… as they discover the true meaning of the ESRI Portal.
- Green must survive this chapter
---
Chapter 4:
- this chapter describes the Green Chipchenko's last stand
- the final battle where Green appies their geospatial expertise to solve the conflict
---
Chapter 5:
- The final chapter
- What remains of Green Chipchenkos world… will it be a new beginning or a lasting servitude to the new world order.

---
Background on Green Chipchenko
Profession: GIS Specialist & Cartographer
Background:  
  - Holds a Advanced Diploma in Geographic Information Systems
  - Works for a government agency monitoring environmental changes via spatial data
  - Renowned for identifying patterns and anomalies in maps
Personality Traits:
  - Detail-oriented, methodical, persistent  
  - Socially awkward but passionate about cartography  
  - Motivated by curiosity and a strong sense of responsibility
Signature Items:
  - Weathered leather-bound field notebook (full of sketches & annotations)  
  - Rugged laptop that often crashes
  - Mysterious compass rumored to point to “truth” rather than north
Motivations:
  - Unravel the cartographic anomaly threatening the world  
  - Prove the critical role of cartography  
  - Overcome skepticism from peers and authorities
---
Key point to consider
  - The ESRI Portal is in name only. It does not need to be the same as the GIS product created by ESRI.
  - Write in the style of Clive Cussler's Dirk Pitt
