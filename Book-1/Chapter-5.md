🗺️ A New World Order (of Coordinates)
What remained of Green’s world was a world cataloged, verified, and fundamentally secure. The victory wasn't military; it was cartographic. The Open Source Prevails movement, once a fringe philosophy of sharing spatial data freely, had been proven to be the linchpin of reality itself. By refusing to lock away crucial geographic information, Green had allowed the truth of the world to be its own defense, preventing the proprietary, closed-source nature of the Portal from gaining a foothold. Now, the government agencies, thoroughly shaken by the realization that geospatial data integrity was the ultimate security measure, fully funded Green’s vision: a global, decentralized, open-source mapping initiative. There would be no more secrets in the spatial data, only transparency, and in that transparency, safety. Green's weathered leather-bound field notebook now held the draft manifesto for the Global Open Map Foundation (GOMF), ensuring lasting servitude only to the truth.
